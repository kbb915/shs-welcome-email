Hi {fullname:first},

Welcome to SpringHill Suites McKinney. We are absolutely thrilled to have you with us.

Our entire team is excited to be part of your stay, and we take great pride in creating a warm, comfortable, and relaxing environment where you can unwind, recharge, and feel right at home.

If there is ever anything you need during your stay, please do not hesitate to reach out. Our team is here for you 24/7 and happy to assist however we can.

Make the most of your stay

Outdoor Pool & Fireplace

Enjoy a refreshing swim in our outdoor pool, open daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM. When the sun sets, relax by our cozy fireplace right next to the pool area. It is a perfect spot to unwind.

Fitness Center

Stay on top of your routine in our fully equipped 24-hour fitness center.

Ice Machines

Ice machines are available on every floor. When you step out of the elevator, they are located just to the left.

Local Dining

We are conveniently located within walking distance of several great bars and restaurants.

View nearby options

Feel free to ask us for recommendations. We love sharing our local favorites.

Free Guest WiFi

Stay connected with complimentary WiFi.

  1. Select the "Marriott Bonvoy Guest" network.
  2. Choose "Already a Member."
  3. Enter your last name and room number in the dropdowns. Membership is not required.

Complimentary Breakfast

Join us for breakfast in the lobby:

  • Weekdays: 6:30 AM to 9:30 AM
  • Weekends: 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM

Parking

Parking is $2 per day. Please stop by the front desk for a parking pass.

Housekeeping

We offer housekeeping every other day. If you need fresh towels, toiletries, coffee, or anything else, just stop by the front desk and we will gladly accommodate.

We are honored to have you with us and are committed to making your experience a great one. Thank you for choosing SpringHill Suites McKinney. We hope you enjoy every moment of your stay.

Warmly,
The SpringHill Suites McKinney Team
